# Test shortcuts (developer build only)

These only exist in builds made with `EZEUS_DEV_CHEATS=ON` (the
`eZeus-dev.exe` that `eZeus-tools\build.cmd` makes). They act on the city
you are viewing.

| Keys | What it does |
|---|---|
| Ctrl+Shift+M | +10,000 drachmas |
| Ctrl+Shift+W | Fills storehouses with marble, wood, sculpture, orichalc, black marble, armor and bronze (up to 32 each, as space allows) |
| Ctrl+Shift+F | Makes every sanctuary available (and lifts the scenario's sanctuary limit), then instantly finishes every sanctuary and monument under construction |
| Ctrl+Shift+Y | Unlocks every Atlantean pyramid and monument in the build menu |
| Ctrl+Shift+G | An angry god attacks next day (Ares, Artemis, Zeus, Poseidon, ... in turn) |

Typical test: place the building, press Ctrl+Shift+F to finish it.
